# My Favorite Movie: *Escape Room* 

*Escape Room* is a **2019 psychological thriller** directed by Adam Robitel. The film centers around six strangers who are invited to participate in a series of escape rooms, but soon discover that the stakes are much higher than they anticipated.

## Plot Summary
A group of strangers is invited to take part in an immersive escape room experience, with a chance to win a large sum of money. However, they quickly realize that the rooms are not just a game but are life-threatening.

### Main Characters
- **Zoey** (Taylor Russell)
- **Jason** (Logan Miller)
- **Amanda** (Deborah Ann Woll)
- **Ben** (Jay Ellis)
- **Mike** (Tyler Labine)

---

## Themes
- **Survival**
- **Psychological Games**
- **Trust and Betrayal**
- **The Human Spirit**

> *"It's not a game. It's a life or death situation."* — Zoey
